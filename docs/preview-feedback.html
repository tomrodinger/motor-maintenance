Success feedback — six options

Click the buttons. Each option is shown on a motor card and a table row, because whatever we pick has to read well in both. All six are ~0.6–0.75 s, GPU-only (no layout shift), and collapse to nothing under prefers-reduced-motion.

My recommendation: 1 + 3 together.

Option 1 is the only one that confirms which command you sent, because the acknowledgement happens on the control you touched — and with four icon buttons per row that matters. Option 3 says which motor at a glance and is thematically right: the tool's whole job is talking to devices with status LEDs, so echoing that language is free meaning. Together they answer both questions in under a second without a single pixel of layout shift. Options 2 and 5 are prettier in isolation but draw the eye harder than a routine success deserves, and 5 gets busy when you fire several in a row. Option 6 is the best of all of them for commands that change a value — set-alias especially — so I would use it in addition, not instead.
